Condition plates: 26 across six regions

Engraved intaglio on warm ivory, Patent construction geometry or Guilloche rosette, tissue coloured by standard anatomical convention. Each region shows the plates full size, then at real medallion size beneath.

The system

Anatomical plates show the structure and its pathology at rest. Functional plates show how the joint works or fails, rendered in the same engraving: the structure drawn in two or three overlapping positions with earlier ones in lighter line, dashed motion arcs, and load expressed as hatching density rather than added colour.

Colour is never decorative. Variety comes from the tissue itself, so a nerve condition reads yellow, a muscle condition reads red, a cartilage condition reads pale blue.
