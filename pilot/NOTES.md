# Pilot Illustration Systems

All final pilots are square 1254 x 1254 PNG files. Each final was opened at full size and checked again at 264 x 264 for anatomy, pathology signal, sibling consistency, and circular-crop safety.

## Cabinet Dissection

Cabinet Dissection uses warm graphite and sepia tonal modelling with controlled cross-hatching, giving each structure the authority of a commissioned medical natural-history plate. Pathology is shown through real morphological change, such as torn fibers, focal thickening, extrusion, or nerve flattening, with antique gold confined to the affected tissue.

Hard cases: diffuse pain syndromes, movement-control diagnoses, centrally mediated symptoms, or early conditions without a defensible visible tissue change. These should use an anatomically relevant focal structure without inventing a lesion.

## Section & Signal

Section & Signal uses economical orthographic cutaways, broad paper, and precise engraved linework to preserve maximum clarity at medallion scale. Pathology is a flat gold clinical signal reinforced by a crisp structural event, such as discontinuity, compression, focal thickening, or displacement.

Hard cases: multi-structure syndromes, dynamic instability, and conditions whose defining relationship is strongly three-dimensional. Selecting one section plane can oversimplify those conditions.

## Anatomical Relief

Anatomical Relief renders each subject as a shallow ivory plaster or paper relief with graphite recesses, creating a quiet museum-specimen object rather than a conventional drawing. Pathology is indicated by physical deformation or fiber damage with a matte gold inlay following the affected tissue.

Hard cases: transparent or nested structures, very thin bursae and labra, subtle branching nerves, and lesions that depend on small differences in tissue color or depth. The shared relief material can reduce tissue distinction when anatomy becomes crowded.

## Recommendation

Choose **Cabinet Dissection** for the 56-page production system. It best matches the medical-journal and natural-history-library brand, provides enough context to remain clinically credible across bone, tendon, ligament, fascia, disc, and nerve conditions, and keeps the gold accent purposeful without making the site feel diagrammatic or decorative.

Section & Signal is the strongest fallback when absolute small-scale clarity and production economy outweigh tonal richness. Anatomical Relief is distinctive and premium, but it is less adaptable when a condition depends on transparent layering or very small tissue relationships.
