What is wrong today

Eleven plates cover fifty-six conditions. All fourteen foot and ankle pages share one image; all nine shoulder pages share another. Plantar fasciitis and a bunion get the identical picture.

The existing plates come from different sources, so the knee is a warm graphite wash and the foot is a cold stipple engraving cropped off mid-bone. No frame can make them a set.

And none of them show the condition — only a generic region.
