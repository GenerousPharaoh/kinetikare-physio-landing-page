# Pilot 2 Illustration Systems

All final images are square 1254 × 1254 PNG files. Each accepted image was opened at full size and inspected again at 264 × 264 through a circular mask for anatomical relationships, pathology location, gold-signal clarity, and crop safety.

## Gilded Repair

Gilded Repair treats anatomy as a pale ceramic museum object whose physical vulnerability is revealed by a restrained kintsugi intervention. The antique-gold seam, fissure, or compression inlay is the pathology itself; diffuse inflammation, non-structural pain, instability without a discrete lesion, and widespread neural symptoms would be difficult because the repair metaphor demands one defensible focal defect.

## Patent Plate

Patent Plate renders the body as a nineteenth-century mechanism under examination, using precise line weights, section hatching, and faint unlabelled construction geometry. Antique gold isolates the genuinely affected component while discontinuity, thickening, or flattening supplies the pathological morphology; diffuse pain syndromes, centrally mediated symptoms, and conditions defined mainly by movement would be hardest to reduce to one static plate.

## Herbarium

Herbarium presents each structure as a dry mounted natural-history specimen on aged paper, held with minute gold pins or hair-fine thread. The diseased tissue itself turns antique gold while the mounting devices establish specimen logic; very deep structures, dynamic impingement, subtle edema, and conditions that become visually unpleasant when treated as dissected specimens would be difficult.

## Letterpress

Letterpress presses anatomy into thick cotton paper so form emerges from crushed fibers, shallow relief, and raked shadow rather than drawn colour. Gold foil is struck only into the torn, thickened, or compressed tissue; thin bursae, small labral lesions, branching nerves, low-contrast cross-sections, and multi-layered anatomy would be difficult because blind emboss can collapse fine tissue distinctions at 264 px.

## Celestial

Celestial charts bones and soft tissues as disciplined constellation forms on deep ink, retaining anatomical relationships within the grammar of an antique star atlas. A single antique-gold star and faint halo mark the pathological focus while a broken, bunched, or flattened line pattern supplies the structural diagnosis; diffuse or multi-site conditions, broad tendinopathy, and pathologies that cannot honestly be reduced to one point would be difficult.

## Guilloche

Guilloche builds anatomical volume from swelling and tapering banknote-intaglio lines, with a quiet security rosette behind the specimen. The injured tissue alone is engraved in antique gold and retains its rupture, thickening, or compression morphology; subtle lesions, densely layered regions, large soft-tissue fields, and conditions requiring colour separation would be difficult because fine line density can merge at small size.

## Recommendation

Choose **Patent Plate** to carry the 56-page condition library. It offers the best balance of creative distinction, anatomical legibility, cross-section handling, tissue flexibility, and repeatable production logic, while the construction geometry creates a coherent system without competing with the gold pathology signal.

Guilloche is the strongest alternate when richness and collecting-object character matter more than production economy. Celestial is the most memorable campaign system, but its single-star grammar is too restrictive for a complete clinical library.
