Six creative systems for the condition pages

Further from conventional medical illustration, same accuracy bar. Three conditions each, chosen to break things: an intra-articular ligament, a fascia attachment, and a cross-section.

Codex's recommendation: Patent Plate for the 56-page library — best balance of distinction, legibility, cross-section handling and repeatable production logic. Guilloche is the alternate when richness matters more than economy. Celestial is the most memorable but its single-star grammar is too restrictive for a full clinical set. No system was dropped; revisions corrected crop safety, medial-versus-lateral fascia placement, compressed-nerve visibility and carpal tunnel tendon counts.
