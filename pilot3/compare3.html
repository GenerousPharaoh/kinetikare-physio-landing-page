Round three: function-forward, palette open

Two changes from the first two rounds. The gold-on-parchment constraint is lifted, and every system was judged on whether it communicates how a joint works or fails rather than only what it looks like.

Codex's recommendation: Load Path for the 56-page library, with Scan Slice as the fallback for conditions that have a clear structural correlate but no honest single load path. Its production caution is worth keeping: the colour field should show relative concentration, never imply measured patient-specific values.

Load Path

Codex's pick

The only system whose grammar answers a functional question on every page: where load travels, where it concentrates, and how the pathology interrupts it. The plantar fascia plate reads instantly, cool blue along the band resolving into a red bloom exactly at the calcaneal enthesis where the pain is. Adapts to ligament tension, enthesis traction, nerve compression, joint contact and tendon load.
